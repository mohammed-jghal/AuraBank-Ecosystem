# Data

- `customers.json` — 100 synthetic customer profiles, generated by
  `scripts/generate_data.py` with seed 42. Zero PII: ids are
  `customer-001` … `customer-100`, no names, no national ids, no
  addresses, no phone numbers. Labels (`label_loan_approved`,
  `label_fraud`) are synthetic ground truth for measurement only.
- `policies/` — loan, fraud, and KYC policy documents. These are the
  RAG source; edit the policy here and the agents retrieve the new
  wording without a code change.
- Regenerate with `make data`. Do not commit real customer data here.
